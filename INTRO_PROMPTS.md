# AVEGEE — ภาพบทนำ 5 หน้า

ตอนนี้เกมใช้ภาพตัวละคร/ฉากเดิมประกอบบทนำอยู่แล้ว ไฟล์ใหม่เหล่านี้เป็นภาพทดแทนในรอบ art pass ไม่จำเป็นต่อการเล่นเวอร์ชันนี้

## กติกากลาง (ใส่ต้น prompt ทุกภาพ)

Create one finished panel for a five-page introductory comic to the Thai 2D pixel-art game “AVEGEE”. Dark-fantasy Thai afterlife, warm ember highlights against deep plum and charcoal shadows, hand-crafted chunky pixel art with crisp readable silhouettes. Match the existing AVEGEE character sprites and landscape perspective. Wide landscape composition, 16:9, 1536×864 or higher. Leave a dark, uncluttered strip along the bottom 22% for dialogue added by the game UI. No letters, no words, no subtitles, no speech balloons, no logos, no UI, no watermarks. Do not copy the visual design of any other game. Keep recurring characters consistent across all five panels: Yama is a small young red-skinned horned underworld judge wearing a gold Thai crown and dark robe; Nira is a pale lilac-skinned horned archivist holding a stack of files; the father is a tall imposing red-skinned king of the underworld in black-and-gold royal armor. The tone is mysterious and humane, not gore or horror.

## 01 — สาขาที่ถูกทิ้งไว้

An establishing view of a neglected Thai afterlife branch: a cracked stone judgment platform, stacks of sealed case files, a long queue of distant translucent souls arriving across a wooden bridge, a few inactive punishment buildings and low lava channels. Only two tiny staff silhouettes remain in the huge space. The camera is wide and slightly elevated like AVEGEE’s existing 2D map, with the court clearly at the center. Mood: overwhelming work, but a place that can be restored. No prominent characters in the foreground.

## 02 — พ่อมอบตรา

Inside a dim royal judgment hall, the underworld king father places a small golden judicial seal into young Yama’s hands. Father’s posture is stern and controlled; Yama looks anxious but determined. Show a believable family resemblance without making them identical. A shaft of ember light isolates the seal as the visual focal point. The scene must communicate responsibility, not a battle or threat.

## 03 — นิราเปิดแฟ้มแรก

Nira opens the first case file beside young Yama at the stone bench. Above the pages appear two uncertain ghostly impressions of the same person: one admired by the living, one partly hidden in shadow. Do not imply which image is true. Show Yama looking at Nira’s file rather than judging the soul’s appearance. The visual theme is “public image versus concealed truth,” with no literal text on the papers.

## 04 — คำตัดสินมีปลายทาง

A translucent soul walks along a safe stone path away from the judgment platform, escorted by one underworld crew member toward a distant punishment station. Other paths branch toward different buildings. The route must stay visibly on ground or bridge; do not make anyone float across lava. Yama remains at the court in the background, watching the consequence of his order. Avoid showing torture or gore.

## 05 — บัญชีของผู้ตัดสิน

The father stands behind Yama as a shadowed but recognizable figure, while Yama holds a glowing balance scale. One side contains a small case file, the other a dark ember representing excess punishment returning to the judge. Yama is still standing, ready to face the first case; the image should feel like a serious vow, not a Game Over screen. Leave space for the UI’s final “รับงาน” button.
