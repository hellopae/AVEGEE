# AVEGEE — Oriverse 2D/2.5D follow-up, 9 October 2026

Implements the five recommendations accepted in “สำรวจเทคนิคทำเกมจาก Oriverse”. The completed document minigame remains the prerequisite. This change uses the existing Canvas renderer and approved map-v5/theme-v4 artwork.

1. **Front/back occlusion:** `art.blockOf` now uses the measured ground footprint, rather than the roof-to-foot rectangle. Existing `depthOf` sorts buildings at the rear edge of their footprint and actors at their feet. Roofs may hide a player walking behind them. Terrain masks still block water/lava; building service corridors and NPC blockers remain active.
2. **Atmosphere:** Thai frontier adds embers and entrance smoke; Asia adds snow; West adds low mist; Cyberhell adds sparse signal particles. Zone maps retain cached terrain glints and lantern effects, with Asia snow and West mist. Decorative layers do not change navigation. Frontier weather has at most 24 particles and a gate light.
3. **Contact shadows/light:** standees, walking hero and walking crew share a soft three-ellipse foot shadow, also used by battle renderers through art.js. Station ground shadows are now drawn before depth-sorted actors/buildings. Pools of light mark the sala, mirror hall, heaven gate and frontier entrance. Warm Thai/Asia light and cool West/Cyberhell light follow the existing palette. Light gradients are cached 96px sprites, not rebuilt every frame.
4. **Art contract/prototype:** `src/scene-style.js` owns the four palettes and weather profiles. Existing `MAP_REVIEW_SIZES`, `reviewStationBox`, alpha-measured feet and foot-anchored hero sheets define the approved scale and perspective. Use `output/oriverse/review.html` to inspect the same production map/frontier renderers. The sala + path + Yama corner is the reference: “ยืนหลังศาลา” / “ยืนหน้าศาลา” demonstrates occlusion. The page creates a temporary game state and never loads/saves the player's save.
5. **Follow camera:** frontier uses frame-rate-independent exponential easing (130ms), bounded at world edges. It pans only when cover scaling leaves a world larger than the viewport; zone maps retain their overview. Pointer navigation and floating fight/gate/Nira buttons use the same camera box. Resize resets the camera to valid bounds. Reduced motion disables weather and eases no camera; static light remains.
